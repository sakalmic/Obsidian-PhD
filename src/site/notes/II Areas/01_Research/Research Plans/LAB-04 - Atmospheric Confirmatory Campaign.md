# LAB-04 — Atmospheric confirmatory campaign

**Parent:** [[II Areas/01_Research/Research Plans/RESEARCH-00 - Atmospheric Tier 1 Workflow 2026-2028\|RESEARCH-00 - Atmospheric Tier 1 Workflow 2026-2028]]. **Depends on:** [[II Areas/01_Research/Research Plans/LAB-03 - Screening and June Decision Gate\|LAB-03 - Screening and June Decision Gate]]. **Next:** [[II Areas/01_Research/Research Plans/LAB-05 - Independent Prediction and Operating-Choice Check\|LAB-05 - Independent Prediction and Operating-Choice Check]].

## Purpose and frozen design

Acquire the new, shot-level atmospheric evidence for the dissertational probability, delay and channel-state questions. Execute only the matrix, attempt-count/stopping rule and analysis definitions approved at the June gate. Changes require a dated amendment and separate exploratory reporting.

## Session procedure

1. Before each block, verify the approved apparatus configuration, calibration version, optical-arrival correction, ambient sensors and electrode state. Record the ready-state rule and condition order.
2. For each attempt, acquire the electrical trigger, optical pick-off, voltage/current waveforms, measured laser input and selected channel diagnostic on a common time reference where possible. Link them by immutable shot and session IDs.
3. Apply the frozen breakdown criterion and observation window. Preserve no-breakdown attempts as censored records; separately label premature/self-breakdowns, missing optical pulses, saturation and acquisition faults.
4. Interleave or randomise approved settings within safe blocks, monitor environment/electrode drift and record operator/alignment/reset time plus system energy for the later TCO boundary.
5. Repeat the principal contrasts after independent realignment and in a separately initiated session. Keep complete reserved sessions outside model fitting and operating-point selection.
6. Run versioned QC: completeness, timestamps, channel saturation, calibration validity, optical-feature window before breakdown and consistency of attempt counts. Do not inspect held-out outcomes to tune the model or stopping rule.

## Outputs and exit gate

Produce a frozen dataset manifest, calibration links, exclusion/censoring ledger, session-level uncertainty report and reproducible Paper 1 analysis of atmospheric operating behaviour. Distinguish these new data from the May 2024 and February 2025 series in every figure and manuscript.

**Done when:** the planned sample/precision goal is met or a predeclared limitation is reported; all attempted shots have disposition codes; the independent repeat exists; and QC passes without outcome-dependent selection. The remaining held-out sessions stay sealed for LAB-05.
