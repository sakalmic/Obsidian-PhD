# LAB-03 — Screening and June decision gate

**Parent:** [[RESEARCH-00 - Atmospheric Tier 1 Workflow 2026-2028]]. **Depends on:** [[LAB-02 - Laser and Channel Diagnostic Pilot]]. **Next:** [[LAB-04 - Atmospheric Confirmatory Campaign]].

## Purpose

Select a **small atmospheric confirmatory matrix** that tests the scientific question with available beam time. Use one reference geometry, polarity and laser branch. A second geometry/polarity or optical condition is a bounded robustness check only after the core comparison is repeatable and adequately supported.

## Screening sequence

1. Specify candidate factors from measured—not merely nominal—inputs: normalised voltage $k$, delivered laser input and one justified focus/channel condition. Include no-laser or laser-off controls where the protocol permits.
2. Select a compact sequential set spanning near-threshold conditions informative for probability estimation and any higher-performing point required by the predefined laboratory service. Do not launch a full factorial sweep across wavelength, geometry, polarity and pulse regime.
3. Block by day and electrode history; randomise condition order within safe blocks. Keep every ready-state attempted shot and distinguish experimental failures from acquisition failures.
4. Inspect probability, timing, optical descriptor availability, between-session variation, saturation and drift. Document exploratory estimates as screening results, not confirmatory claims.
5. Use observed event rates and session clustering to calculate the required confirmation count from a stated interval-width or power goal. Freeze the stopping rule, effect-size target, primary contrasts, event markers, censoring rules, model inputs and held-out-session assignment **before** LAB-04.

## June stop/go record

The candidate and supervisor should record one of three decisions: **proceed** with the compact matrix; **narrow** the question after a measured diagnostic or repeatability limit; or **pause** confirmation until the apparatus/measurement chain is corrected. Document the scientific contribution that remains feasible. A fallback is not automatic proof of degree sufficiency.

**Done when:** the confirmatory protocol, condition matrix, attempt-count rationale, analysis plan and independent validation reservation are signed off in a dated decision record. Preserve the screened data separately; do not silently relabel them as preregistered confirmation.
