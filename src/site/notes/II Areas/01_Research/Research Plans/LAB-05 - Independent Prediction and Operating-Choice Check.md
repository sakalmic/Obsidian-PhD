# LAB-05 — Independent prediction and operating-choice check

**Parent:** [[II Areas/01_Research/Research Plans/RESEARCH-00 - Atmospheric Tier 1 Workflow 2026-2028\|RESEARCH-00 - Atmospheric Tier 1 Workflow 2026-2028]]. **Depends on:** [[II Areas/01_Research/Research Plans/LAB-04 - Atmospheric Confirmatory Campaign\|LAB-04 - Atmospheric Confirmatory Campaign]]. This is a validation/limited acquisition ticket, not a new broad campaign.

## Before opening reserved data

1. Freeze M0 (voltage, measured laser input and declared context) and M1 (M0 plus the predeclared pre-breakdown channel descriptor), preprocessing, fitted parameters, uncertainty method and performance metrics.
2. Define probability calibration and a proper score; define delay survival probability or a predeclared quantile with censored attempts retained. State which predictions apply to all attempts and which condition on survival to a diagnostic landmark.
3. Freeze the operating-choice rule using training/calibration sessions, the predefined technical requirements and a cost scenario. A descriptor measured after a laser pulse cannot select that pulse's energy; integrate its distribution when predicting performance of a future configuration.

## Independent check

4. Evaluate both models on complete sessions reserved before confirmation. Report results by condition as well as pooled, including missing features, failures and regions of poor calibration. Do not retune the winning model on those results and present them as untouched validation.
5. If needed and still within the bounded atmospheric scope, acquire one separately initiated check session at the **preselected** operating point to test technical feasibility. Use the same calibrated chain, outcome codes and QC rules.
6. Propagate performance uncertainty into bounded TCO and test sensitivity to utilisation, equipment allocation, electricity, operator/reset time, maintenance and diagnostic cost. Keep shared-facility and dedicated-installation cases separate.

## Outputs and exit gate

Deliver a held-out M0/M1 comparison, parameter and sensitivity table, prediction failure map, technical-feasibility result, and the validated response required by [[II Areas/03_Thesis/Claims/CL-07 - Techno-economic operating choice\|CL-07 - Techno-economic operating choice]]. If M1 does not improve prediction or no operating point meets the predefined requirement, report that result without retrospectively relaxing the criteria.

**Done when:** validation and operating-choice analyses can be rerun from frozen records, the use of post-laser information is explicit, and no new core acquisition is needed for the March–April 2028 TCO synthesis. A complete dissertation draft remains targeted for June 2028 and submission for August 2028.
