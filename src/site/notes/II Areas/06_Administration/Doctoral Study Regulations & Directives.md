## Working interpretation for the September 2024 cohort

- The professional discussion/scientific study is part of closing the study block; confirm its exact formal role for the individual record.
- Public FEL guidance states that students in a four-year full-time programme take the State Doctoral Examination by the end of the third study year. The preferred internal target is June 2027.
- SDZ eligibility includes the completed study block and English requirement; verify every item in KOS.
- Internationalisation and publication requirements must be completed before dissertation submission.
- Original results included in the dissertation must be published or accepted for publication according to the applicable rules.
- A monograph or an annotated collection of publications may be possible subject to the current rules and approval.
