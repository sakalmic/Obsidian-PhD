# Dissertation Scope & Decision Log

## Status policy

This register records unresolved decisions; no item below is an approval. Use `proposed`, `confirmed`, `completed` or `superseded`, together with date, authority and evidence. An unchecked action or an export labelled “approved” is not a substitute for a decision record.

| ID | Decision | Current state | Required evidence / next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DEC-01 | Final CZ/EN title and C-A to C-C scope | Proposed | Supervisor/committee decision and registered wording |
| DEC-02 | Adequacy of bounded reliability/economic assessment for the programme | Unconfirmed | Supervisor/programme decision on required economic contribution |
| DEC-03 | Exact professional-discussion date and format | Unconfirmed | Invitation or written confirmation; 24 September is a planning placeholder |
| DEC-04 | Study-block completion, English and SDZ eligibility | Unverified | KOS evidence and applicable individual requirements |
| DEC-05 | Submission target versus binding deadline and defence date | Unverified | KOS/ISP or office confirmation; August 2028 remains the internal submission target |
| DEC-06 | Primary laser, electrical source and diagnostics | Unconfirmed | Laboratory owner, configuration ID, booking and calibration records |
| DEC-07 | December 2026 versus November 2027 IEEE submission | Unresolved | Determine whether the 1 September meeting refers to the same paper; retain the original meeting record |
| DEC-08 | Publication acceptance/indexing and internationalisation requirements | Unverified | Dated programme-specific confirmation and evidence locations |
| DEC-09 | Dissertation language and monograph/collection format | Proposed | Supervisor/programme confirmation |

## Baseline adopted for document organisation, 7 September 2026

- Use the narrow atmospheric-breakdown proposal for current planning.
- Preserve historical research alternatives without treating them as approved obligations.
- Keep experiments and evidence in II Areas; III Resources is reserved for external references.
- Keep the scientific calendar in [[I Projects/02_Campaigns/LTSG Core Research Package 2026-2028#Calendar and milestones\|Core LTSG Research Programme (2026–2028)]].
- Keep administrative gates in [[I Projects/03_Milestones/20260925 Minimum/ISP & Milestone Tracking\|Doctoral Milestones & ISP Tracker]].
- Distinguish submitted, under review, accepted, published and indexed publication states.
