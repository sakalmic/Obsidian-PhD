# Teaching Responsibilities and Courses

Record of teaching commitments at CTU FEE across the Department of Economics, Management and Humanities and the Department of Electrical Power Engineering.

---

## Course overview

### 1. Power-engineering laboratory classes

- **Semester:** WS 2026/2027
- **Workload:** Two hours per week, Monday 14:15–15:45.
- **Content:** Measurements on distribution-line models, switching overvoltages, and transformer protection.

### 2. Seminars on techno-economic assessment of power systems

- **Semester:** SS 2026/2027
- **Workload:** Two hours per week.
- **Content:** Life-cycle cost calculations, reliability models, and the economics of renewable energy sources.
