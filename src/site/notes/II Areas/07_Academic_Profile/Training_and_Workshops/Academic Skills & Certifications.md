# Academic Skills & Certifications

Record of formal qualifications, safety certifications, technical competencies, and specialized summer schools attended during the PhD journey.

---

## 📜 Certifications & Safety Qualifications

- **Laser Safety Officer (LSO):** Certified for Class 4 laser operations (HiLASE Laser Centre & CTU FEE).
- **High-Voltage Safety Certificate (Vyhláška 50 / NV 194/2022 Sb.):** Qualified electrical specialist for experimental setups up to $40\,\text{kV}$.
- **COMSOL Multiphysics Modeling:** Advanced Plasma & High-Voltage Discharges workshop certification.

## 🎓 Summer Schools & Advanced Courses

- **Plasma Physics Summer School:** Fundamentals of streamer discharge kinetics, non-equilibrium air plasmas, and diagnostics.
- **Scientific Writing & Open Science:** Academic publishing in high-impact Q1/Q2 journals (IEEE, PSST).
