# Travel and Conference Funding

Record of funding options for international conferences, workshops, and research visits.

---

## Mobility funding

1. **CTU Mobility Fund**
   - Support for presenting original work at recognised international conferences.
2. **SGS travel budget**
   - Registration, accommodation, and transport funded from the student grant.
3. **Departmental resources**
   - Supplementary support for strategically important international presentations.
