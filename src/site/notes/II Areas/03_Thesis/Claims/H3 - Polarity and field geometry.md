# H3 - Polarity and field geometry

Contribution: supporting-C1. Atmospheric Tier 1 only; the statement remains unverified until linked evidence supports or bounds it. C4 supplies traceability. See [[II Areas/03_Thesis/Claim_Ledger/Claim Ledger & Evidence Matrix\|Claim Ledger & Evidence Matrix]].

## Hypothesis

Polarity or field geometry modifies at least one identified relationship between channel state and breakdown dynamics after configurations are normalised by their separately measured $U_{50}$ values.

## Role in the thesis

This is a supporting robustness question, not a condition for successful completion of the minimum thesis. Begin with one reference geometry and polarity; activate the interaction test only after the core operating window is reproducible.

## Required evidence

- separate WP1 baselines for every compared configuration;
- matched working coefficients and measurement chains;
- predeclared interaction rather than independent within-group significance tests.

## Decision rule

Report an interaction estimate with uncertainty. A null result becomes a quantitative bound and does not trigger an uncontrolled expansion of geometries.
