# P3 - Techno-economic operating choice

## Proposition

Within the validated atmospheric configuration family, technical measurements and traceable cost inputs delimit the set of operating choices that satisfy predefined demand, timing and success requirements, and quantify the economic limits. The admissible set may be empty or its cost ranking unstable; lower cost, a unique optimum and financial savings are not assumed.

## Required evidence

- A frozen laboratory service, technical requirements, observation window and demand/capacity model.
- Independent validation of the selected operating rule and uncertainty in compliant-operation probability.
- Separately documented shared-facility and dedicated-installation cost scenarios.
- Traceable inputs or explicitly labelled ranges; consistent discounting and no double counting.
- Sensitivity, break-even boundaries and the incremental cost of channel diagnostics.

## Decision rule

Select the rule using calibration/training data and a declared cost scenario, then independently test technical feasibility. Report cost ranking with uncertainty. If no point meets constraints, report no demonstrated feasible solution. If rankings change across plausible inputs, report the boundary and information needed, not a single asserted optimum. The ratio TCO/[attempts × compliant probability] does not replace demand or capacity constraints.

## Exclusions

Avoided grid outages, avoided SF₆ costs, complete switchgear qualification and product lifetime benefits require separate system evidence and remain outside the base case.
