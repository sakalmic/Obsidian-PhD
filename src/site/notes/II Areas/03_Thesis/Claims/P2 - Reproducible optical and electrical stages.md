# P2 - Reproducible optical and electrical stages

Contribution: supporting-C1. Atmospheric Tier 1 only; the statement remains unverified until linked evidence supports or bounds it. C4 supplies traceability. See [[II Areas/03_Thesis/Claim_Ledger/Claim Ledger & Evidence Matrix|Claim Ledger & Evidence Matrix]].

## Proposition

Synchronized optical and electrical measurements can resolve a reproducible ordering of observable events relative to calibrated optical time zero when diagnostic resolution is sufficient. Physical stage labels require independent supporting evidence. A laser-to-HV delay scan is conditional on independently controllable HV timing; it is not mandatory for a continuously biased DC setup.

## Required evidence

- common time base and documented timing offsets;
- synchronized channel/emission, voltage and current records;
- laser-to-breakdown timing in the confirmed configuration; an independently controlled HV-delay scan only when technically available and justified;
- stability of the inferred ordering across sessions;
- no stage label based solely on one ambiguous waveform feature.

## Decision rule

Support requires stable event ordering or a predeclared model comparison across an independent repeat. If the diagnostics cannot uniquely identify physical stages, report observable timing classes and their limits rather than assigning mechanism labels.
