## Document lifecycle

1. Define a working proposal and its evidence requirements.
2. Record an approval only with a dated decision and an identifiable authority.
3. Link results to data and analyses before using them in conclusions.
4. Produce presentation/PDF snapshots from the agreed scope and mark their date and status.
5. Retain previous snapshots as historical material; do not use their dates or status labels to override current records.

## Manuscript transition
