# State Doctoral Examination and Minimum Thesis

## Planning assumption

The September 2026 professional discussion fixes the dissertation topic and provides the immediate feedback needed for the scientific study/minimum. For a full-time student who began in September 2024, the internal target is to take the SDZ by June 2027, leaving the end of August 2027 only as contingency.

The exact relationship between the professional discussion, scientific study/minimum and SDZ application must be confirmed in writing for the individual KOS record.

## Immediate project

All content, deliverables, presentation preparation and the three-week schedule are maintained in [[Minimum Dissertation Study & Research Discussion 2026]].

## Eligibility checklist

- [ ] Applicable study-block requirements completed.
- [ ] Required professional courses verified in KOS.
- [ ] English examination/certificate verified.
- [ ] Required publication for the study block accepted and correctly recorded.
- [ ] Scientific study/minimum and professional discussion completed in the required form.
- [ ] SDZ topics selected with the supervisor and approved through the required process.
- [ ] Application, attachments and deadline confirmed with OVV.

## Proposed SDZ preparation areas

Final topics are selected with the supervisor and approved by the programme board. Working preparation areas are:

1. high-voltage engineering, gaseous breakdown and switching transients;
2. laser-induced ionisation, plasma-channel evolution and diagnostics;
3. statistical experimental design, uncertainty and predictive modelling;
4. reliability and economic evaluation of electrical-engineering systems where required by the programme.

## Working schedule

| Period | Output |
| --- | --- |
| Sep 2026 | Professional discussion, topic freeze and recorded revisions |
| Oct-Dec 2026 | Expand literature/theory sections; close study-block gaps |
| Jan-Feb 2027 | Complete minimum/study document and verify eligibility |
| Mar 2027 | Supervisor review and application package |
| Apr-Jun 2027 | Focused preparation and preferred examination term |
| Jul-Aug 2027 | Administrative contingency only if required |

## Evidence to retain

- official written requirement confirmation;
- submitted application and attachments;
- approved examination topics;
- final scientific study/minimum;
- presentation and committee minutes;
- examination result and KOS record.
