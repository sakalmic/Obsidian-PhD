### 1.3 Hypotheses and Falsification Rules

* **Scientific Hypothesis $H_1$ (Channel-Driven Breakdown):**  
  In the sub-microsecond regime following a picosecond laser pulse, the breakdown trigger probability $P_{\text{trig}}$ and the mean formation delay $\bar{t}_d$ correlate strictly with the localized neutral gas density depletion $\Delta N(r,t)/N_0$ and the resulting peak reduced electric field $(E/N)_{\text{max}}$, displaying statistically superior predictive power over models driven solely by laser pulse energy $E_{\text{pulse}}$.
* **Null Hypothesis $H_0$ (Energy-Threshold Equivalence):**  
  Knowledge of time-resolved optical channel geometry or gas density provides no statistically detectable improvement in predicting $P_{\text{trig}}$ or $t_d$ compared to a standard logistic model parameterized exclusively by the applied DC voltage $U_{\text{app}}$ and nominal laser pulse energy $E_{\text{pulse}}$.
* **Strict Falsification Gate:**  
  If an independent cross-validation dataset (unseen electrode gap or shifted focal plane) demonstrates that the root-mean-square error (RMSE) of delay prediction or the cross-entropy of breakdown probability is not reduced by at least 15% ($p < 0.01$) when incorporating measured channel descriptors over $(U_{\text{app}}, E_{\text{pulse}})$ alone, $H_1$ is rejected, and the thesis pivots to the robust probabilistic operational model (Variant A).

### 2.1 Scientific Gap Beyond Prior Work
The candidate's Master's thesis (2024) established static breakdown voltage reductions ($\Delta U \approx 20\%\text{--}31\%$) using the PERLA picosecond laser platform at 1030 nm, 515 nm, and 257.5 nm across 5 mm and 10 mm gaps up to 40 kV. Subsequent 2025 APL conference measurements observed discharge delays from $0.16$ to $8.1\,\mu\text{s}$. However, in common with broader literature, these measurements exhibited three unresolved scientific limitations:
1. **Systematic Time-Origin Error:** Delays were referenced to the electrical trigger command of the laser oscillator rather than the physical arrival of the photon packet in the gap.
2. **Black-Box Irradiance Assumption:** Laser input was characterized by set-point energy, ignoring shot-to-shot beam waist fluctuations, air plasma defocussing, and non-linear optical losses.
3. **Equifinality of Delay Mechanisms:** Literature conflates two distinct physical regimes without time-resolved separation:
   * **Direct Plasma-Seeded Transition ($t_d < 50\text{ ns}$):** Governed by remnant free electrons promoting instant streamer avalanches.
   * **Thermal/Hydrodynamic Transition ($t_d > 100\text{ ns}$):** Governed by hot-core acoustic expansion creating a rarefied gas channel ($\Delta \rho / \rho_0 < 1$), where reduced gas density $N$ dramatically enhances Townsend's first ionization coefficient $\alpha/N = f(E/N)$.

### 2.2 Microscopic Physics and Governing Formulations

#### Phase I: Optical Initiation and Carrier Generation ($t < 1\text{ ns}$)
In atmospheric air, optical breakdown by picosecond pulses involves multiphoton ionization (MPI) as the initiator, followed by electron avalanche ionization:
$$\frac{\partial n_e}{\partial t} = \sigma_k I^k + \alpha_{\text{opt}} I n_e - \eta_{\text{att}} n_e - \beta_{\text{rec}} n_e n_i$$
where $I$ is laser irradiance, $\sigma_k$ is the multi-photon cross-section, $\alpha_{\text{opt}}$ is the inverse bremsstrahlung absorption coefficient, $\eta_{\text{att}}$ is oxygen attachment, and $\beta_{\text{rec}}$ is dissociative electron-ion recombination.

#### Phase II: Thermalization and Hydrodynamic Channel Expansion ($10\text{ ns} < t < 10\,\mu\text{s}$)
Rapid thermal equilibration transfers energy from the electron population to the neutral gas, generating an acoustic blast wave:
$$\rho \frac{\partial \mathbf{u}}{\partial t} + \rho (\mathbf{u} \cdot \nabla) \mathbf{u} = -\nabla p, \quad \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} + \nabla \cdot (\rho \mathbf{u}) = 0$$
The expanding cylindrical pressure wave leaves behind a heated, low-density channel along the optical axis:
$$N(r,t) = N_{\text{ambient}} \left[ 1 - \Delta_{\text{core}}(t) \exp\left(-\frac{r^2}{r_{\text{core}}^2(t)}\right) \right]$$
where $\Delta_{\text{core}}(t)$ reaches peak rarefaction within 100–500 ns, directly elevating the local reduced electric field:
$$\left(\frac{E}{N}\right)(r,t) = \frac{E_{\text{applied}}(r)}{N(r,t)}$$

#### Phase III: Streamer Formation and Electrical Breakdown
Breakdown triggers when the Meek-Raether avalanche-to-streamer criterion is satisfied along the modified path $\Gamma$:
$$\int_{\Gamma} \left[ \alpha\left(\frac{E(x)}{N(x,t)}\right) - \eta\left(\frac{E(x)}{N(x,t)}\right) \right] dx \ge K \approx 18\text{--}20$$
The stochastic switching delay $t_d$ is thus the sum of the statistical inception time $t_{\text{stat}}$ (waiting for an initiatory electron in the critical volume) and the formative time $t_{\text{form}}$ (avalanche propagation and channel heating to arc transition):
$$t_d = t_{\text{stat}} + t_{\text{form}}$$

### 2.3 Key Scientific Challenges
1. **Deconvolution of Multi-Timescale Physics:** Separating remnant optical ionization from acoustic gas rarefaction. This requires systematic laser-to-voltage delay scanning or time-resolved Schlieren probing.
2. **Parameter Identifiability in Reduced Modeling:** Ensuring the reduced physical model does not overfit via unconstrained effective parameters. Model parameters must map to measurable physical properties ($r_{\text{core}}$, $\Delta N/N_0$, $E_{50}$).
3. **Stochastic Censoring of Misfires & Pre-fires:** In experimental regimes near the threshold ($k = U_{\text{app}}/U_{50} \approx 0.5\text{--}0.7$), shots exhibit both non-fires ($t_d \to \infty$) and spontaneous self-breakdowns ($t_d < 0$). Standard Gaussian statistics fail; survival analysis with right-censoring must be applied:
   $$S(t) = P(t_d > t), \quad h(t) = -\frac{d}{dt} \ln S(t)$$

### 3.1 High-Voltage and Fast Electrical Metrology
* **Optical Time-Zero ($t_0$) Implementation:**  
  A high-bandwidth InGaAs/Si photodiode ($t_{\text{rise}} < 100\text{ ps}$) sampling beam leakage behind the final turning mirror establishes $t_0$ directly at the entrance window of the spark chamber. Total coaxial line delays must be calibrated via time-domain reflectometry (TDR) with sub-100 ps precision.
* **Voltage Collapse Profiling ($dV/dt$):**  
  The breakdown instant is defined as the point where the gap voltage collapses through 50% of the pre-shot steady-state level:
  $$t_{\text{breakdown}} = \left. t \right|_{V(t) = 0.5 \cdot V_0}$$
  The Tektronix P6015A high-voltage probe (rated 75 MHz, 15 kV DC / 40 kV pulse) must be checked for line compensation, cable dielectric losses, and grounding loop inductance. An auxiliary capacitive divider ($C$-divider) integrated into the chamber flange will provide an independent, high-frequency voltage reference ($> 300\text{ MHz}$).
* **Current Sensor Upgrade (Pearson 1423 Limitation):**  
  The Pearson 1423 current transformer cited in historical records has a manufacturer-rated rise time of $500\text{ ns}$ and an upper cutoff of $\approx 0.7\text{ MHz}$, rendering it incapable of resolving nanosecond discharge rise times.  
  *Remedy:* Deployment of a calibrated low-inductance coaxial resistive current-viewing shunt (CVR, rise time $< 200\text{ ps}$) or an ultra-fast Pearson Model 2878 / 6585 ($t_{\text{rise}} < 2\text{ ns}$) in series with the return ground path.

### 3.2 Optical Diagnostics & Laser Beam Delivery
* **Laser Platform Verification (HiLASE PERLA B):**  
  Operating at 1030 nm and harmonics (515 nm, 257.5 nm), producing picosecond pulses ($1\text{--}10\text{ kHz}$ or single-shot external trigger).  
  *Diagnostic Challenge:* Eliminating shot-to-shot energy fluctuations ($> 3\%$) and thermal lensing. Each recorded shot must acquire the individual pulse energy via a calibrated pyroelectric energy meter.
* **Time-Resolved Shadowgraphy / Schlieren Train:**  
  To measure the acoustic blast wave radius $r_{\text{blast}}(t)$ and core density depletion $\Delta N(t)$, a continuous-wave (CW) 532 nm probe laser or a synchronized auxiliary pulsed probe paired with a fast-gated ICCD or high-speed CMOS camera will record transverse shadowgrams at programmed delays ($10\text{ ns} \le \Delta t \le 10\,\mu\text{s}$) post-trigger.
* **Beam Waist & Focal Positioning:**  
  Precise motorized 3-axis stage manipulation ($< 10\,\mu\text{m}$ resolution) ensuring the beam waist $w_0$ is centered within the inter-electrode axis without impinging on the metal electrode surfaces, preventing premature laser-ablation triggering.

### 3.3 Environmental and Electrode Drift Control
* **Atmospheric State Acquisition:**  
  Continuous digital logging of temperature ($T \pm 0.1\,^\circ\text{C}$), absolute ambient pressure ($P \pm 0.5\text{ hPa}$), and relative humidity ($\text{RH} \pm 1.5\%$) to compute the standard air density correction factor $\delta = (P/P_0)(T_0/T)$ for baseline $U_{50}$ normalization.
* **Electrode Surface Evolution:**  
  Repetitive arc discharges erode the spherical electrode surfaces. An optical profilometer will map electrode crater depth every 500 shots. Test blocks will alternate between fresh and conditioned states to isolate environmental drift from surface erosion.

### WP0: Instrumentation, Metrological Audit & Commissioning

### WP1: Baseline Statistical Characterization & Stochastic Delay Mapping
* **Timeline:** Months 04–07 (December 2026 – March 2027)
* **Objective:** Establish the probabilistic switching window and statistical delay distributions for reference spherical geometry.
* **Tasks:**
  1. *Task 1.1:* Execute systematic self-breakdown tests to determine $U_{50}$ and Weibull/logistic parameters as a function of air density $\delta$.
  2. *Task 1.2:* Map trigger probability $P_{\text{trig}}(k, E_{\text{pulse}})$ and delay distributions across working coefficients $k = U_{\text{app}}/U_{50} \in [0.4, 0.95]$ and energy levels.
  3. *Task 1.3:* Quantify jitter ($\sigma_{t_d}$ and median absolute deviation) applying right-censored survival analysis.
* **Output:** Dataset D1, Claim C-A baseline, State Doctoral Exam (SDZ) written minimum submission.

### WP2: Time-Resolved Channel Diagnostics & Physical Mechanism Isolation
* **Timeline:** Months 08–12 (April 2027 – August 2027)

* **Tasks:**
  1. *Task 2.1:* Align transverse shadowgraphy / Schlieren imaging system synchronized with variable laser-to-probe delays ($10\text{ ns} \le \Delta t \le 10\,\mu\text{s}$).
  2. *Task 2.2:* Extract acoustic shock radius $r_{\text{blast}}(t)$ and core gas density rarefaction profiles $\Delta N(r,t)/N_0$.
  3. *Task 2.3:* Correlate optical channel diameter and density drop with electrical delay $t_d$ under identical electrical stress.
* **Output:** Dataset D2 (shadowgraphy & optical channel parameters), Claim C-B evidence package.

### WP3: Reduced Predictive Modeling & Parameter Identification

* **Tasks:**

  2. *Task 3.2:* Implement parameter identification using calibration subsets; execute sensitivity analysis (Sobol indices) to verify parameter identifiability.
  3. *Task 3.3:* Prepare and submit **Journal Manuscript 1** (focus: experimental characterization, optical channel dynamics, and timing distributions).
