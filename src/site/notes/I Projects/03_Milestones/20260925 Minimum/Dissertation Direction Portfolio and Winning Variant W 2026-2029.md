# Dissertation Direction Portfolio and Winning Variant W, 2026–2029

> [!summary] Decision status
> This document compares all currently defined dissertation directions and proposes **W** as the preferred integrated route. It is a decision-support document, not evidence that the title, collaboration, apparatus access or authorship plan has been approved. Approval and later scope changes must be recorded in [[II Areas/06_Administration/Supervisor_Sync/Doctoral Scope & Decision Register\|Doctoral Scope & Decision Register]].

## Executive recommendation

The recommended direction is:

**Channel-State-Informed Prediction and Diagnostics of Laser-Assisted Breakdown in Air and Fluorine-Free CO₂ Insulation Systems**

Variant **W** combines the strongest scientific core of [Direction B](/direction-b-channel-state-and-prediction-of-switching/) with the industrial transition addressed by [Direction O1](/direction-o1-sf6-free-diagnostics/) and a deliberately bounded part of [Direction O2](/direction-o2-fluorine-free-media/). Its central question is whether measured properties of a laser-created plasma channel improve prediction of breakdown probability, delay and jitter beyond nominal voltage and laser energy, and whether the resulting method transfers from atmospheric air to a controlled CO₂ insulation environment.

The work must not claim that CO₂ alone is a universal drop-in replacement for SF₆ or that a laboratory gap qualifies complete switchgear. The defensible result is a validated measurement and prediction method, a transfer study between two media, and a documented operating domain. This provides a rigorous dissertation even if the application demonstrator remains limited.

The collaboration opportunity is concrete but must be formally agreed. Iva Burýšková's 2026 Master's thesis, *Investigating CO₂ Streamer Inception in Repetitive Pulsed Discharges*, reports sphere–sphere measurements at 1.5, 3 and 3.75 mm and 0.10–0.35 MPa. This creates a useful reference for CO₂ streamer inception, pressure-cell practice and repeated-pulse behaviour. Variant W can add laser-created channel diagnostics and predictive validation without duplicating or absorbing the contribution of that thesis. [CTU repository record](https://dspace.cvut.cz/entities/publication/32c12fec-82b5-479e-9941-7a889816237c)

## Decision criteria

All directions are assessed against six criteria:

1. **Scientific contribution:** a falsifiable claim suitable for peer review.
2. **Continuity:** use of the existing laser-triggered spark-gap apparatus, diagnostics and accumulated knowledge.
3. **Feasibility:** access to equipment, calibration, safety and sufficient repetitions.
4. **Schedule resilience:** a defensible core remains available if an extension fails.
5. **Application value:** a defined user, decision or qualification problem.
6. **Completion by 2029:** data collection, publications and dissertation synthesis have a hard stop no later than December 2029.

## Direction assessments A–F

### A — Reliability and robust operating window

**Question.** Where is laser-assisted breakdown reproducible when normalized voltage, environment and electrode state are controlled?

**Evidence and apparatus.** A calibrated atmospheric-air gap, confirmed laser configuration, voltage/current channels, an optical time marker and environmental logging are sufficient. The analysis must retain failures and censored delays and must include an independent repeat.

**Schedule and application.** A complete operating map is achievable during 2026–2027 and can support laboratory HV triggering and qualification of optically isolated trigger systems. A is the minimum fallback for W if channel-state diagnostics or the CO₂ branch fail.

### B — Channel state and prediction of switching

**Question.** Does a measured descriptor of channel formation predict breakdown better than pulse energy and voltage alone?

**Evidence and apparatus.** A synchronized gated image, calibrated optical-emission signal, density proxy or another physically justified channel descriptor is added to the A stack. Prediction must be scored on sessions or operating conditions excluded from calibration.

**Schedule and application.** The air-based dataset and first predictive model should be frozen by the end of 2027, with independent validation and a manuscript in 2028. B supplies the scientific centre of W and enables predictive setup control and model-based trigger optimization.

### C — Adaptive control and operational utility

**Question.** Can an early-time or pre-shot feature select a safe setting that achieves target switching probability with lower energy or delay?

**Evidence and apparatus.** This requires validated B features, low-latency processing, bounded control authority and an independent safety watchdog. Offline policy evaluation must precede any closed-loop test.

**Schedule and application.** C is feasible by 2029 only after B is complete. It is an optional extra for W, not part of its minimum thesis claim.

### D — Spatial shaping of the channel

**Question.** Does one verified shaped-beam geometry improve path control or repeatability at matched delivered energy?

**Evidence and apparatus.** An axicon or other justified optic, beam profiling, spatially resolved imaging and controlled energy matching are required.

**Schedule and application.** A bounded comparison can fit into 2027–2028, but it competes for optical alignment time and does not directly strengthen the CO₂ transfer question. It should enter W only if shaping solves a measured channel-continuity limitation.

### E — Synchronization of two gaps

**Question.** Can a common optical source reduce relative timing error and joint misfire probability across two gaps?

**Evidence and apparatus.** Two matched gaps, common timing, optical splitting and enough independent acquisition channels are needed.

**Schedule and application.** A demonstrator is feasible by 2029, but it moves the thesis from medium transfer toward system architecture. Retain it as a later application of the W predictor, not a core work package.

### F — Dielectric recovery and component wear

**Question.** How do switching conditions and electrode history affect post-discharge recovery and repeated operation?

**Evidence and apparatus.** Recovery-voltage measurement, controlled repetition, electrode imaging or profilometry, temperature monitoring and a frozen conditioning protocol are needed.

**Schedule and application.** A bounded recovery metric could be added in 2028–2029 after the W transfer study. It is the most natural technical extension if repeated CO₂ pulses reveal memory or surface effects.

## Direction assessments O1–O8

### O1 — Optical diagnostics for SF₆-free switchgear

O1 targets condition monitoring or qualification: a calibrated optical feature must distinguish controlled dielectric or discharge states and be tested blindly or on held-out data. It has strong market relevance, but a partner-defined use case and approved test cell are essential. W adopts the diagnostic principle while keeping the core claim at laboratory-cell level.

### O2 — Breakdown and recovery in fluorine-free media

O2 compares breakdown inception and recovery across media and pressures. It offers the strongest direct connection to the CO₂ work, but a broad gas-mixture campaign would be too large. W therefore restricts the mandatory comparison to **air plus one CO₂ configuration family**, with further mixtures activated only after the principal model is validated.

### O3 — Probabilistic digital twin

O3 maps uncertain physical inputs to reliability or maintenance decisions. It becomes defensible only when supported by a versioned experimental dataset and partner-defined system boundary. In W, a compact probabilistic surrogate may be an extra deliverable; a full switchgear digital twin is outside the required scope.

### O4 — Hybrid crowbar

O4 provides a clear system demonstrator and measurable let-through energy, trigger success and recovery. It is commercially legible but requires substantial high-current engineering. It should not displace W's medium-transfer dataset before 2029.

### O5 — Multi-gap photonic synchronisation

O5 scales optical distribution to three or more gaps. It is attractive for Marx generators and arrays, but it adds hardware and joint-reliability complexity. It is a post-thesis or partner-funded continuation unless multi-gap access is already confirmed.

### O6 — Lightning attachment

O6 transfers channel lifetime and geometry knowledge to leader inception or attachment. The scientific impact may be high, but the facility, scaling and safety dependencies make it unsuitable as the required W core before 2029.

### O7 — Long-lived luminous structures

O7 investigates a reproducible laboratory plasma or aerosol structure without claiming reproduction of natural ball lightning. Its mechanism risk and diagnostic breadth are high. It should remain a strictly time-boxed exploratory study outside W.

### O8 — Hydrogen/ammonia ignition

O8 applies plasma-state measurements to low-carbon-fuel ignition. It has a clear application, but a certified combustion facility and different safety and chemistry expertise are required. It is technically coherent with channel-state prediction but does not support the SF₆-replacement objective.

## W — Winning integrated direction

### Proposed title

**Channel-State-Informed Prediction and Diagnostics of Laser-Assisted Breakdown in Air and Fluorine-Free CO₂ Insulation Systems**

Alternative shorter title:

**Predictive Diagnostics of Laser-Assisted Breakdown in Air and CO₂ for SF₆-Free High-Voltage Insulation**

### Central research question

Can measured time-dependent properties of a laser-created plasma channel predict breakdown probability, delay and jitter more accurately than voltage and laser energy alone, and which parts of that predictive relationship remain valid when the method is transferred from atmospheric air to controlled pressurized CO₂?

### Testable hypotheses

- **H1:** at least one calibrated channel descriptor provides out-of-sample predictive information beyond normalized voltage and pulse energy in air;
- **H2:** the direction and magnitude of this predictive gain can be quantified in CO₂ under a matched, documented geometry;
- **H3:** a medium-aware reduced model can identify both a valid operating region and failure regions without treating optical intensity as an uncalibrated proxy for electron density or temperature;
- **H4, optional:** one optical feature can classify or bound a controlled insulation/discharge state relevant to fluorine-free equipment.

Failure to support a hypothesis remains a publishable result if the detection limit, uncertainty, operating domain and independent repeat are reported.

### Original contribution

W is intended to deliver four linked contributions:

1. a reproducible air-based relationship between channel state and stochastic switching response;
2. a held-out comparison of an energy/voltage baseline model with a channel-informed model;
3. a controlled transfer study in CO₂ that distinguishes general channel effects from medium-specific behaviour;
4. a bounded diagnostic or qualification method relevant to research on SF₆-free insulation.

This is distinct from the earlier CO₂ work: the proposed contribution is not another table of breakdown voltages. It introduces synchronized laser-channel observables, probabilistic prediction, transfer testing and an explicit validation protocol.

### Tiered execution plan

#### Tier 1 — Base: defensible dissertation core

**Scope:** A + B in atmospheric air.

**Required outputs:** calibrated metrology; frozen air dataset; probability/delay/jitter model; held-out validation; independent repeat; at least one submitted scientific paper and thesis chapters on method and channel-state prediction.

**Completion target:** December 2027 for the principal dataset; June 2028 for the validated model and manuscript.

**Fallback:** if no channel descriptor is sufficiently repeatable, complete A as a rigorous operating-window dissertation contribution and report the diagnostic limit.

#### Tier 2 — Extension: recommended winning scope

**Scope:** Tier 1 plus transfer to CO₂ and a bounded O1/O2 result.

**Required outputs:** approved pressure-cell protocol; matched CO₂ reference states; laser-assisted CO₂ dataset; comparison of predictor structure and performance between air and CO₂; one diagnostic or qualification-oriented result; second manuscript or a substantial integrated thesis chapter.

**Completion target:** December 2028 for data; June 2029 for independent repeat and publication submission.

**Fallback:** if pressure-cell or collaboration access is not confirmed by March 2028, retain W as the air-based B core and convert the CO₂ branch into a documented postdoctoral or grant proposal.

#### Tier 3 — Extra: only after the thesis evidence is secure

Choose at most one:

- a compact uncertainty-aware digital twin derived from O3;
- a recovery/memory experiment derived from F;
- an offline adaptive decision rule derived from C;
- a partner-facing diagnostic demonstrator derived from O1.

**Completion target:** June 2029 for data and September 2029 for final analysis. No new experiment begins after June 2029 unless it directly closes a documented thesis evidence gap.

### Minimum apparatus

- existing atmospheric-air LTSG with controlled electrode geometry;
- laser-energy, focus and beam-profile measurement;
- calibrated voltage and current diagnostics with a common time base;
- fast photodetection and at least one synchronized channel-state diagnostic;
- environmental and electrode-history logging;
- approved sealed CO₂ cell with pressure and temperature measurement;
- documented gas handling, leak testing, ventilation and pressure safety;
- reproducible analysis environment for hierarchical probability, delay/survival and uncertainty models.

Gated imaging, schlieren/interferometry or spectroscopy should be included only when calibrated and tied to a decision. A large chemistry model, many gas mixtures and complete switchgear qualification are excluded from the mandatory scope.

### Experimental design

Use a staged design that protects confirmatory data:

1. define normalized voltage and the primary response before screening;
2. preserve misfires, censored delays and all valid shots;
3. separate screening conditions from confirmation and validation conditions;
4. block or randomize by session, electrode history, pressure and medium;
5. compare a baseline predictor using voltage and laser settings with a model that adds channel descriptors;
6. evaluate calibration, discrimination and prediction error on untouched sessions or operating points;
7. repeat the principal air and CO₂ results independently;
8. publish the valid domain and failure map, not only the best operating point.

### Expected publications and thesis architecture

- **Paper 1:** channel-state-informed prediction of laser-assisted breakdown in air;
- **Paper 2:** transfer of the measurement and prediction framework to CO₂;
- **Optional Paper 3 or demonstrator report:** one Tier-3 application after the core is secure;
- **Thesis chapter 1:** physical background and SF₆-transition motivation;
- **Thesis chapter 2:** apparatus, metrology, uncertainty and reproducibility;
- **Thesis chapter 3:** air operating window and channel-state model;
- **Thesis chapter 4:** CO₂ transfer, medium dependence and diagnostic implications;
- **Thesis chapter 5:** limits, application pathway and validated design rules.

### Application pathway

The near-term technical outputs are a measurement protocol, diagnostic feature library, predictor and operating/failure map. Potential users include switchgear R&D laboratories, gas-mixture developers, test laboratories and manufacturers evaluating eco-efficient insulation. A commercial path could lead to a diagnostic module or qualification workflow, but product-readiness, lifetime and economic claims require partner tests beyond the dissertation cell.

The European timing supports the relevance of completion by 2029. Regulation (EU) 2024/573 introduces switchgear restrictions in stages, including up to 24 kV from 2026, parts of the 52–145 kV range from 2028, 24–52 kV from 2030 and higher voltage classes from 2032, subject to the regulation's conditions and derogations. This makes a validated 2028–2029 research method timely without requiring the dissertation itself to deliver a market-ready replacement. [Consolidated EUR-Lex text, Article 13](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/573/2024-02-20/eng/)

### Principal risks and controls

| Risk | Early indicator | Control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Channel diagnostic does not add predictive value | No repeatable descriptor by August 2027 | Complete A; publish a quantitative negative result |
| CO₂ cell or collaboration is delayed | No written access by March 2028 | Do not activate Tier 2; protect the air core |
| Too many pressures, geometries or gases | Matrix exceeds available confirmatory shots | Restrict to air and one CO₂ configuration family |
| Optical signals are overinterpreted | No calibration or forward model | Treat them as empirical descriptors with stated limits |
| Electrode conditioning confounds medium effects | Session/electrode block dominates outcomes | Randomize, log history and model hierarchical effects |
| Application work delays thesis | Core paper or chapter slips at a gate | Freeze extras and return resources to Tier 1/2 |
| Contribution overlap is unclear | Shared data without ownership record | Agree roles, data stewardship and authorship before acquisition |

### Definition of done

Variant W is complete when:

- the air-based primary claim is supported, rejected or bounded on held-out data;
- the principal result has an independent repeat;
- the CO₂ transfer result is completed or formally removed at the March 2028 gate;
- every reported quantity has traceable calibration and uncertainty;
- data, code, exclusions and failure regions are documented;
- at least two coherent paper-level outputs or equivalent accepted/submitted contributions support the thesis;
- the dissertation is frozen and submitted no later than December 2029.

## Final selection statement

W is preferred because it preserves the feasibility of B, adds a timely industrial question, and treats collaboration as a modular extension rather than a dependency that could invalidate the whole doctorate. Tier 1 alone remains a defensible dissertation core. Tier 2 produces the intended scientific and commercial differentiation. Tier 3 is valuable only after the evidence for the thesis is secure.
