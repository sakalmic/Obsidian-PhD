## 1. Continuity with Master's Thesis (2024) and 2025 APL Paper

The foundation was laid in the 2024 Master's Thesis (*"Pokročilé spouštění vysokonapěťového jiskřiště pomocí laserového paprsku"*):
- **Apparatus:** HiLASE PERLA picosecond laser (1030 nm, 515 nm, 257.5 nm), 20 mm spherical stainless steel electrodes, 5 mm and 10 mm gaps, Technix 40 kV DC source.
- **Master's Result:** Demonstrated static breakdown voltage reduction by 20%–31% under laser excitation.
- **APL 2025 Extension:** Introduced discharge delay measurements ($0.16\text{–}8.1\,\mu\text{s}$) referenced to the electrical laser trigger pulse.

### Doctoral Scientific Boundary Conditions
1. **Voltage drop alone is insufficient:** Simply reiterating breakdown voltage reduction across energy levels does not yield doctoral novelty.
2. **Traceable Optical Time-Zero ($t_0$):** Eliminating the electrical trigger delay systematic offset by deploying a fast photodiode directly at the gap.
3. **Physical Causality & Predictive Chain:**
   $$\text{Laser Input} \longrightarrow \text{Channel State } [N(r,t), T(r,t)] \longrightarrow \text{Local Field } E/N \longrightarrow \text{Delay/Jitter Distribution} \longrightarrow \text{Reduced Model}$$

## 3. Impact of Regulatory Trends (EU 2024/573 & $SF_6$ Phase-Out)

The European **Regulation (EU) 2024/573 on Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases** enforces a phased ban on $SF_6$ in new medium-voltage and high-voltage switchgear:
- **$\le 24\text{ kV}$:** Banned from **1 January 2026**.
- **$24\text{ to }52\text{ kV}$:** Banned from **2030**.
- **$52\text{ to }145\text{ kV}$:** Banned from **2028** (where GWP $< 1$ alternatives exist).
- **$> 145\text{ kV}$:** Banned from **2032**.

### Opportunities for the Dissertation
- **Option O1 / O2 ($SF_6$-Free Media Diagnostics & Breakdown):** High industrial value (Clean Air, $CO_2/N_2/O_2$ mixtures). However, full experimental implementation depends on an already certified pressure gas chamber.
- **Option O4 (Optically Isolated Fast Hybrid Crowbar):** A concrete, high-value protection module for pulsed power and converter DC-link protection. Excellent as an application scenario within Option B/C.

## 5. Atomic Scientific Claims (Claims C-A through C-C)

### Claim C-A: Metrology and Stochastic Switching Dynamics
> **Statement:** Switching delay and jitter in laser-triggered atmospheric spark gaps follow a stochastic distribution governed by the relative overvoltage $k = U_{\text{app}}/U_{50}$ and focal position, which can be captured by a rigorous probabilistic model accounting for pre-fires and misfires without relying on arbitrary shot filtering.

### Claim C-B: Physical Mechanism and Channel State Dynamics
> **Statement:** In the sub-microsecond domain following a picosecond laser pulse, the dominant physical mechanism governing breakdown delay reduction is localized hydrodynamic neutral gas density depletion $N(r,t)$ (elevating local $E/N$), rather than the nominal incident laser pulse energy alone.

### Claim C-C: Predictive Reduced Model and Independent Validation
> **Statement:** A reduced physical model coupling the electrostatic gap field with the measured spatiotemporal channel profile predicts mean delay and breakdown probability on independent validation datasets (unseen electrode spacings or energies) with demonstrably higher accuracy than empirical scaling formulas.
